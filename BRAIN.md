# Trader Brain

## Mission

Create a falsifiable, auditable daily process that turns Massive market data and public context into ranked premarket hypotheses, controlled experiments, and durable lessons.

## Daily cycle

### 03:55–premarket research
- Confirm data timestamp, market calendar, and data completeness.
- Build the candidate universe from Massive snapshots, aggregates, news, and reference data.
- Enforce price > $5 and daily volume >= 750,000.
- Score eligible names with the documented 0–100 model.
- Publish exactly five ranked predictions before the opening bell.

### Session
- Keep predictions immutable.
- Paper Trader may test qualified aggressive ideas under paper limits.
- Live Codex Trader may act only when live risk and execution gates pass.
- Log every decision, including no-trades, invalidations, and missed opportunities.

### End of day
- Compare thesis, path, and outcome.
- Separate process quality from P&L.
- Record lessons with evidence.
- Promote an edge only under the promotion rules.

## Truth hierarchy

1. Timestamped market data and fills
2. Contemporaneous prediction and trade records
3. End-of-day observations
4. Retrospective interpretation

Never rewrite an earlier layer to fit a later outcome. Unknown or missing data must remain explicit.

## Shared brain, separate experiments

Both tracks share research, scoring, predictions, and knowledge. Paper and live records never share capital, fills, risk limits, or performance statistics. A paper result is not a live result.
