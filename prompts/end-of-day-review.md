# End-of-Day Review Prompt

Use the frozen Top 5, Massive intraday data, paper ledger, live ledger, and decision notes.

For each prediction and trade:
- Reconstruct the path without hindsight edits.
- Compare thesis, trigger, invalidation, maximum favorable/adverse excursion, close, and outcome.
- Separate prediction quality, execution quality, risk compliance, and P&L.
- Identify missed opportunities and avoided bad trades.
- Name data gaps and rule deviations.
- Write one concrete keep/change/test action.

Update the daily review and lessons learned. Propose—but do not automatically add—a promoted edge unless promotion criteria are met.
