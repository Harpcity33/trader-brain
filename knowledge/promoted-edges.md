# Promoted Edges

An edge may be promoted only when it has:
- A precise, testable definition
- A predeclared outcome metric
- Sufficient independent observations across multiple sessions/regimes
- Costs and realistic fills included
- Counterexamples and failure conditions documented
- Positive evidence that is not driven by one outlier
- A review date and owner

Status values: proposed, testing, promoted, paused, retired. Promotion changes require a dated entry and links to evidence. No edge bypasses live risk rules.
