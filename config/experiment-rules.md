# Experiment Rules

## Paper Trader

Purpose: explore aggressive or high-upside hypotheses safely.

- May test qualified setups that pass the hard screener gates.
- Must label the hypothesis, variant, expected mechanism, and success criterion.
- Uses realistic fills and separate performance reporting.
- A failed paper test is useful evidence; hiding it is not permitted.

## Live Codex Trader

Purpose: evaluate disciplined execution under tighter controls.

- Uses the same morning Top 5 but applies live risk rules.
- May choose no trade.
- Cannot inherit paper fills, sizing, or performance.
- Any deviation from plan is logged at decision time.

## Comparability

For each experiment track, record sample count, setup version, scoring version, costs/slippage, outcome metric, maximum adverse/favorable excursion, and rule deviations. Do not promote conclusions from small or cherry-picked samples.
