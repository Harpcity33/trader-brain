# 0–100 Scoring Model

Score only candidates that pass every hard screener gate.

| Component | Points |
|---|---:|
| Liquidity and execution quality | 15 |
| Relative volume and participation | 15 |
| Premarket price structure and VWAP/levels | 20 |
| Catalyst and contextual relevance | 15 |
| Prior-move behavior / historical analog | 10 |
| Market, sector, and peer alignment | 10 |
| Risk/reward and invalidation clarity | 15 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Anchors

- 90–100: exceptional, complete evidence; rare
- 80–89: strong, actionable setup
- 70–79: qualified watchlist candidate
- 60–69: interesting but incomplete
- Below 60: do not place in Top 5 unless explicitly marked as a low-confidence research hypothesis

## Rules

- No catalyst is not an automatic zero.
- Missing or stale data reduces the relevant component and must be disclosed.
- A score never overrides hard gates or risk rules.
- Break ties by execution quality, then invalidation clarity.
- Record component scores so the total is reproducible.
